What went wrong, and what we did

Problems we hit during the live night

  1. The rate portal went down for three rounds→ quote from the airline price sheets and mailed bids instead
  2. A customer wanted insurance as its own line; we sent one total→ the quote now follows the price layout the customer asked for
  3. “Don't book yet” in Korean read as a yes→ negations are checked in every language before any booking
  4. Lookalike senders posed as real customers→ sender check: impostors never get a price
  5. An airline's rate desk never answered→ safety net: every request gets an answer by 5:20
Then the rate portal asked for a CAPTCHA 5U's test portal put a CAPTCHA in front of every price lookup. Our agent was locked out of its main price source. How we solved it: we'll tell you.
Why it holds up

Rules first, and a safety net we tested

One AI call per requestThe model only reads. Prices, checks and bookings are code, so they're testable and cheap.
Every failure became a testPrompt injection, impostor senders, insurance in the price, a portal outage: all caught by tests before the next round.
It never books a price nobody acceptedNever two quotes in one thread, never an answer to an impostor.
When unsure, it asksOne short question, or a person. Never a guess.
