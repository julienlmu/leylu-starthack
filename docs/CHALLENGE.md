## The challenge

- For the next 24 hours your team acts as a startup that has just signed its first customer, "SUMI" (spelling uncertain \[?\]).
- SUMI distributes a fictional game, "Grand … 6" (a GTA 6 parody \[?\]), for Popstar Studios. It goes on sale **Sunday at 8 PM** (in-story deadline), and your job is to make sure everything runs cleanly until then.
- Two products: a Standard Edition and a Collector's Edition (with leaflet, statue, etc.). Each is built from parts coming from different suppliers and packing lines.
- Process: a buyer orders in SAP; suppliers check inventory (or source from next-tier suppliers), confirm, and ship; deliveries are tracked, received, packed, and distributed.
- Today only the purchase order lives in SAP. Everything else runs through spreadsheets, emails, and phone calls, and some shipments are not even in the master data.
- 6 suppliers worldwide (communicating in different languages, e.g. Chinese and Polish) and a warehouse in Hamburg. Shipments move by sea and air.
- Expected disruptions: late deliveries, cargo stuck in a port, wrong parts or wrong versions, and external events (geopolitics, earthquakes). The customer's ask: **"no more surprises."**

## Deliverables

- Prototype (deployment optional, but nice to have)
- Pitch deck
- Demo video
- The solution must show **agentic behavior**.
- Any stack and any model are allowed. You also decide the business model.
- The data **changes throughout the weekend** via events.
