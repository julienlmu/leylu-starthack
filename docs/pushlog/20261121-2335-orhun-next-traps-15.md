- What changed (each switch default on, `off` = before):
  - `COUNTER_OFFER`: "We'd accept at 1,800 EUR" / "OK for EUR 1,900, go ahead" was read as a yes and booked our price. A yes that names another amount than the quoted total (or its EUR value) now goes to a person; our own amount written back stays a yes.
  - `WEEKDAY_DEADLINE`: "needed by Friday 28.11.", "must arrive by Friday", "bis Freitag" are deadlines now (first such day after the ready date). Budget wording "can't pay more than 2000 euros", "not over EUR 2,000", "max 2.000,- EUR".
  - `FEET_GRAMS`: dimensions in feet (4 x 3 x 2 ft, 4' x 3' x 2') and weights in grams (500 g) are read and converted; the model may answer ft / g.
  - `AIRPORT_TYPOS`: ICAO codes (EDDF, KORD), lowercase codes (fra) and misspelt cities (Frankfrut, Munchen, Chicgo, Shanghi) become the airport we fly. A misspelt 3-letter code (MCU) is never guessed.
  - `DG_VEHICLES`: engines, used cars, motorcycles, e-scooters, petrol generators, gas cartridges, hair spray, bleach are DG; parts, helmets, tyres are not.
  - `LANGUAGE_RULES`: the reply language follows the customer's own text: "please reply in English" wins, a Chinese/Japanese/Korean/Arabic/Cyrillic body wins over an English signature (R74), a CJK code that came from an attachment falls back to English. The model's field description now says the same.
