- What changed (agent/router.py is_spam, on top of #140/#150):
  - a mail is spam when it has a spam sign and neither a hard shipment fact (number + unit, dims, IATA pair in capitals, packing list/SLI/commercial invoice, "three pallets") nor request wording ("please/could you … quote/offer/price/rate", rfq, Angebot, devis, cotización, preventivo, offerte, teklif/fiyat). Soft freight words alone ("our air freight rates", "your shipment is on hold") no longer protect spam
  - new spam signs: freight newsletters/promos, customs and parcel phishing, 419 scams, sales pitches, job applications, calendar invites, German/French/Spanish/Italian/Turkish spam words, link-only mails; out-of-office and bounces from unknown threads are spam even with a logo attachment
  - an attachment protects unless the mail is phishing/job/scam; "bank details" only counts as phishing with update/confirm/change/verify
