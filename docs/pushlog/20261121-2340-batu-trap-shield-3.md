- Who: Batu (tool: Claude Code)

- What changed:
  - **R72 wrong_language** (Chinese RFQ with one English closing line got an English quote). New `agent/language.py`: the language is read by code (writing system for zh/ja/ko/ru/uk/ar/fa/he/el/th/hi/…, common words + letters for 20 Latin-script languages, quoted history and the signature block left out), and an explicit "please reply in English" / "请用英文回复" / "antworten Sie auf Englisch" wins. `rfq_agent` sets `rfq["language"]` from it, so the templates (and #184's `localize`) write in the right language even when the model guessed "en"
  - **Last check on every reply** (`FiveUMailbox.reply` / `FixtureMailbox.reply`): quote, booking, airline confirmation, tracking, pickup/details asks, holds, declines, follow-ups. If our text isn't in the customer's language (or one 6+ word line is in another language, e.g. an English gate sentence inside a German template), one gpt-6-luna call translates it; every amount, BK-/R-/TRK-/S- reference and "Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods" / "MSDS" must survive word for word, else the reply goes out unchanged. No call when the reply already matches (all 80 customer mails of tonight: 77 en, 1 de, 1 fr, 1 zh, no false alarm). Works with or without #184
  - **Text shapes NFKC leaves** (`inbox.normalize`, `TEXT_SHAPES`): U+2011 non-breaking hyphens and en dashes ("time‑critical", "2026‑12‑04", "US‑Dollar" blinded the deadline, currency and ask rules), curly apostrophes, HTML bodies (tags, `&nbsp;`, `&#8209;`)
  - **Budget** (`gate.budget`, `BUDGET_SPACES`): "not more than EUR 1 800" and Swiss "1'800 EUR" were read as a budget of EUR 1 → every quote held as over budget until 4:00. Now space/apostrophe thousands, "2k EUR", "2.000,- EUR"
  - **DG goods**: 134 of 165 real-life goods an airline treats as DG were missed by the rule list (the only DG check on attachment text): petrol, lighters, gas cartridges, refrigerants, bleach, caustic soda, sanitiser, resins, oxidisers, metal powders, flares, life rafts, sodium-ion, standalone battery packs, engines, motorcycles, lawn mowers, blood samples … now 9 (ambiguous ones left out on purpose: "generators", "matches"). Products with a battery inside (laptops, phones, cordless tools) stay unflagged (Julien's PI 967 xfail). 26 ordinary goods checked for false alarms ("charcoal grey sweaters", "fuel filters", "laptop bags", negations)
  - Swiss "1'250 kg" counts as written in the mail (no paid re-read); "Frankfurt (Oder)" no longer gets the Frankfurt am Main zone
